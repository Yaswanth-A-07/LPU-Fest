🌎 About LPU Fest

Welcome to Lovely Professional University and the amazing LPU Fest 2026.

The festival celebrates culture, creativity and innovation, bringing students together for a week full of energy and excitement.

Students from different countries come together every year to celebrate the spirit of unity in diversity.
